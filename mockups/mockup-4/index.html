OUR MISSION

Angel Tree Twin Lakes is a community-driven program that connects generous sponsors with children in need. We believe every child deserves a magical holiday, and together we make that happen. Your participation helps create moments of joy and wonder that last a lifetime.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple Steps to Make a Difference

1.

SPONSOR A CHILD

Browse children's wishlists and choose to sponsor a full wishlist for one child. You'll be their holiday hero!

2.

PURCHASE GIFTS

Buy the items from the wishlist. When you click "Purchase This Gift," you're donating to the program—not taking it home. You're giving the gift to a child in need.

3.

MARK AS PURCHASED

Update your progress in the app so coordinators know what's been purchased. Keep track in "My Wishlists."

4.

COORDINATOR DELIVERS

Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver all gifts by the Gift Deadline. Your generosity reaches the children!

⚠️ IMPORTANT: All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline set by your coordinator!

BECOME A SPONSOR

As a sponsor, you can:

  • ✓ Sponsor a full wishlist for one child—become their holiday hero!
  • ✓ Purchase extra gifts to help fill gaps and ensure every child gets everything they need.
  • ✓ Track your purchases in "My Wishlists" to see your progress.

Remember: All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline to ensure timely delivery!

TIMELINE & EXPECTATIONS

Important dates to remember:

  • 📅 Gift Deadline: Set by your coordinator (typically early December). This is your deadline!
  • ⏰ Purchase and mark items as purchased as soon as possible—don't wait!
  • 🎁 Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver before the holidays.

💚 The sooner you purchase, the better! Coordinators need time to collect and wrap.

FOR COORDINATORS

You're the backbone of our program! Here's what you do:

📅

SET GIFT DEADLINE

Define the deadline for gift purchases in your area. Make it clear and visible!

👥

ASSIGN SPONSORS

Match sponsors with children and manage wishlist assignments. Keep everyone organized!

🎁

FILL GAPS

Identify and fill any gaps in wishlist coverage. Make sure every child gets everything!
