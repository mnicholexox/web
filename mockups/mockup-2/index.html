Bring Joy to Children This Holiday

A simple, meaningful way to make a difference. Sponsor a child's wishlist or purchase gifts to help our community.

Sponsor a Child Purchase a Gift

Our Mission

We connect generous sponsors with children in need, ensuring every child receives thoughtful gifts during the holidays. Through community collaboration, we create moments of joy that last a lifetime.

How It Works

1

Sponsor a Child

Browse children's wishlists and choose to sponsor a full wishlist for one child.

2

Purchase Gifts

Buy the items from the wishlist. When you click "Purchase This Gift," you're donating to the program—not taking the item home.

3

Mark as Purchased

Update your progress in the app so coordinators know what's been purchased.

4

Coordinator Collects & Delivers

Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver all gifts by the Gift Deadline.

All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline set by your coordinator.

Become a Sponsor

As a sponsor, you can:

  • — Sponsor a full wishlist for one child
  • — Purchase extra gifts to help fill gaps
  • — Track your purchases in "My Wishlists"

All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline to ensure timely delivery.

Timeline & Expectations

Important dates:

  • — Gift Deadline: Set by your coordinator (typically early December)
  • — Purchase and mark items as purchased as soon as possible
  • — Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver before the holidays

For Coordinators

Coordinators manage the program and ensure every child receives their gifts.
