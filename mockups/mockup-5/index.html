Our Mission

Angel Tree Twin Lakes connects generous sponsors with children in need, ensuring every child receives thoughtful gifts during the holidays. Through our community's collective effort, we create moments of joy and wonder that last a lifetime.

01

How It Works

1

Sponsor a Child

Browse children's wishlists and choose to sponsor a full wishlist for one child. You'll be committing to fulfill their entire holiday wishlist.

2

Purchase Gifts

Buy the items from the wishlist. When you click "Purchase This Gift," you're donating to the program—not taking the item home. You're giving the gift to a child in need.

3

Mark as Purchased

Update your progress in the app so coordinators know what's been purchased. Track everything in "My Wishlists" to see your impact.

4

Coordinator Delivers

Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver all gifts by the Gift Deadline. Your generosity reaches the children just in time for the holidays.

All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline set by your coordinator.

02

Become a Sponsor

Your Role

As a sponsor, you can choose how to help:

  • — Sponsor a full wishlist for one child, becoming their holiday hero
  • — Purchase extra gifts to help fill gaps and ensure every child gets everything they need
  • — Track your progress in "My Wishlists" to see your impact

All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline to ensure timely delivery.

Timeline & Expectations

Important dates to remember:

  • — Gift Deadline: Set by your coordinator (typically early December). This is your deadline for all purchases.
  • — Purchase and mark items as purchased as soon as possible—don't wait until the last minute.
  • — Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver before the holidays, ensuring every gift reaches its destination.
