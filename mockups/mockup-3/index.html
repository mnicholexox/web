Once Upon a Holiday

In a community called Twin Lakes, there lived generous hearts who believed every child deserved a magical holiday. This is their story—and yours too.

Begin Your Story Join the Magic

The Mission

Like pages in a beloved storybook, we connect generous sponsors with children in need. Each wishlist becomes a chapter, each gift a moment of wonder. Together, we write a story of joy, hope, and community that children will remember forever.

How the Story Unfolds

1

The Beginning

You discover a child's wishlist, filled with hopes and dreams. With a click, you become their sponsor, committing to fulfill their entire wishlist.

2

The Journey

You purchase each gift with care. Remember: "Purchase This Gift" means you're donating to the program—creating magic for a child, not taking it home.

3

The Progress

In "My Wishlists," you mark each item as purchased. The app becomes your storybook, showing your progress as the magic unfolds.

4

The Delivery

Before the Gift Deadline, coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver every gift. The story reaches its happy ending as children discover their wishes have come true.

✨ All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline to ensure the story ends on time ✨

Your Role in the Story

As a sponsor, you can choose your path:

  • 📖 Sponsor a full wishlist for one child, becoming the hero of their holiday story.
  • 🎁 Purchase extra gifts to help fill gaps and ensure every chapter is complete.
  • 📚 Track your progress in "My Wishlists" as your story unfolds.

Remember: All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline to ensure timely delivery.

The Timeline

Every good story has a timeline:

  • 📅 The Gift Deadline: Set by your coordinator (typically early December). This is when your chapter must be complete.
  • ⏰ Purchase and mark items as purchased as soon as possible—don't wait until the last page!
  • 🎄 Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver before the holidays, ensuring the story ends with joy.
