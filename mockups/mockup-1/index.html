Our Mission

Angel Tree Twin Lakes connects generous sponsors with children in need, ensuring every child receives thoughtful gifts during the holidays. Through our community's collective effort, we create moments of joy and wonder that last a lifetime.

How It Works

1

Sponsor a Child

Browse children's wishlists and choose to sponsor a full wishlist.

2

Purchase Gifts

Buy the items from the wishlist. "Purchase This Gift" means you're donating to the program—not taking it home.

3

Mark as Purchased

Update your progress in the app so coordinators know what's been purchased.

4

Coordinator Collects

Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver gifts by the Gift Deadline.

All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline set by your coordinator.

Become a Sponsor

As a sponsor, you can:

  • • Sponsor a full wishlist for one child
  • • Purchase extra gifts to help fill gaps
  • • Track your purchases in "My Wishlists"

Remember: All gifts must be purchased by the Gift Deadline to ensure timely delivery.

Timeline & Expectations

Important dates:

  • • Gift Deadline: Set by your coordinator (typically early December)
  • • Purchase and mark items as purchased as soon as possible
  • • Coordinators collect, wrap, and deliver before the holidays
